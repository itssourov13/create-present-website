Frequently asked questions

How is this delivered?＋

After payment you get a private link and a QR code. Share the link on WhatsApp or print the QR on a card — it opens the interactive journey in any browser, no app needed.

Do I need to install anything?＋

No. It works in any modern browser on phone, tablet or desktop. It looks best on a phone.

Is my personal content private?＋

Yes. Your journey lives at a private, unguessable link served over a secure, encrypted connection — it’s never listed or shared, and you can permanently delete it and its photos anytime. Only the people you send the link and PIN to can open it.

What is the secret PIN for?＋

It’s a playful lock screen — the recipient enters the 4-digit PIN you chose to “unlock” their surprise. Remember to tell them the PIN.

Can I preview before paying?＋

Yes — you can play the entire journey for free before you decide. You only pay to publish a shareable link.

Can I edit it after publishing?＋

Yes. Open your dashboard, hit Edit on a published journey, make your changes and save — the same link updates instantly with no extra charge.

How long does the link last?＋

Your published journey stays live for 1 year from the day you publish it. You can manage or delete your journeys anytime from your dashboard.

How long do I have access to my journey?＋

Every journey you publish is accessible for 1 year from its publish date. Well before it lapses, you can always create a fresh one from your dashboard.

What photos and music can I add?＋

Upload your own photos (we optimize them automatically so it loads fast) and add a song via a direct audio file link or upload. Spotify and YouTube page links won’t play — download the audio and upload it, and only use music you have the right to share.

What happens if I lose the link?＋

No worries — sign in with the same Google account you published with and every journey you’ve created is right there in your dashboard, with the link and QR ready to copy again.

Is my payment secure?＋

Payments are processed by Razorpay. We never see or store your card details — only a payment reference.

Do you offer refunds?＋

Because each journey is a personalized digital product delivered instantly, refunds are limited — but if something went wrong, email us within 7 days and we’ll make it right. See our Refund Policy for details.

Can I delete it later?＋

Yes. You can permanently delete a journey (and its uploaded photos) from your dashboard at any time — the shared link stops working immediately.
