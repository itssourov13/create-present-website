Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

This policy explains what we collect, why, and your choices. We aim to collect as little as possible.

What we collect

  • Account: your email and basic profile from Google sign-in.
  • Journey content: the recipient name, messages, wishes, PIN, and any photos you upload.
  • Payment: payment is processed by Razorpay. We store only payment/order identifiers — never your card details.

How we use it

  • To create, store, display, and let you manage your journeys.
  • To process payments and prevent fraud.
  • To understand and improve how the product is used.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. We share only with service providers that run the product on our behalf: Supabase (database, storage, authentication) and Razorpay (payments). A published journey's content is visible to anyone who has its link.

Storage & retention

Content is stored with Supabase. We keep a journey until you delete it. Unpaid drafts may be removed automatically after a short period.

Your rights

  • Delete: you can delete any journey (and its photos) from your dashboard at any time.
  • Access/erasure: contact us to access or remove your personal data.

Children

The service is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.
